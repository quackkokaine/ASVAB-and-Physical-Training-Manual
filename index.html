Study smart

Stay hydrated

Keep water at your desk. Even mild dehydration makes it harder to focus and remember.

Get a full night's sleep

Sleep is when your brain locks in what you learned. Aim for 7 to 9 hours, especially before test day.

Study in short blocks

Work for 25 to 45 minutes, then take a 5 to 10 minute break. Stand up and stretch.

Eat real meals

Have a balanced meal or snack before you study. A steady stomach keeps your energy steady.

Put the phone away

Silence notifications while you work. Every interruption costs you several minutes of focus.

Review your mistakes

Redo every missed problem and write down why you missed it. That is the fastest way to improve.
